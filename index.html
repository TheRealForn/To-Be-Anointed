To Be Anointed

A holy warrior, a plagued kingdom, three competing truths

Overview

A 3D pixel art action RPG set in a dark medieval world ravaged by plague. The player is an Ithanian knight — a holy warrior of an ancient sovereign order. Three factions hold competing truths about the plague's origin, and faction allegiance shapes the story's ending.

Design Pillars

  1. Fluid weighty melee combat — every strike has commitment
  2. Faction allegiance shapes story and world fate
  3. Dark medieval atmosphere with moral ambiguity
  4. Open world — castles, ruins, varied biomes
  5. 3D pixel art with modern lighting and atmosphere

Factions

Ithania player start

Empire-like sovereign order, religious holy-warrior iconography. Cross motif central to identity. Loyal, militant, ancient. They believe their faith holds the world together against plague corruption.

Palette: deep red, dark steel, white, gold trim.

Faction Two name tbd

Rebel renegades who claim to know the plague's true origin. Mistrusted by Ithania, hunted by authorities and the infected alike.

Faction Three name tbd

Mystic nature-bound order. View the plague as a natural reckoning rather than a curse.

Enemies

Plague creatures are marked by orange glowing infection spots at joints, eyes, and wounds.

The Hollow

Common shambler. Slow, numerous, introductory. Teaches crowd management.

The Skorn

Fast predator. Aggressive, mid-tier. Punishes poor positioning.

The Wretched

Boss tier. Region-defining encounters.

Dragons

Late-game. Natural and plague-infected variants.

Combat

Fluid but never floaty. Every animation commits — no spam, no mashing. The player thinks in terms of windows: when to strike, when to dodge, when to hold.

Rendering

3D geometry rendered at 640×360, scaled up so the scene reads as pixel art. Cel shader on environment, outline shader via depth/normal edge detection.

Pipeline

Character animation: Blender → FBX → Cascadeur → FBX → Godot. FBX throughout — preserves skinning correctly.
