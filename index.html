To Be Anointed

A holy warrior, a plagued kingdom, three competing truths

Working Draft · Subject to Change

Overview

The current direction for To Be Anointed is a 3D pixel art action RPG set in a dark medieval world ravaged by plague. The intent is for three factions to hold competing truths about the plague's origin, with faction allegiance shaping the story's outcome. The player's starting faction and final role are still being explored.

Everything in this document is a working concept. Details, names, and systems are still being explored and will change.

Current Design Pillars

  1. Fluid weighty melee combat — every strike has commitment
  2. Faction allegiance shapes story and world fate
  3. Dark medieval atmosphere with moral ambiguity
  4. Open world — castles, ruins, varied biomes
  5. 3D pixel art with modern lighting and atmosphere

World

The current concept is a kingdom hollowed out by plague, with the plague's origin contested between factions. Specifics of the setting — geography, history, scale — are still being explored.

Atmosphere Modes (current concept)

Planning to support multiple atmospheric states tied to regions or story moments:

  • Fog — plagued lands (current default in-engine)
  • Bright sunny — possible Ithanian heartlands look
  • Overcast with rain — possible stormy moors look
  • Night — likely late-game / dragon territory

Factions

Three factions is the current plan. Their identities, names, and roles are being developed. Player starting allegiance is undecided — that's part of why there are three.

Ithania defined

The only faction that's solidly defined so far. Empire-like sovereign order with religious holy-warrior iconography. Cross motif is a central identity element. Tone: loyal, militant, ancient. Their belief is that their faith holds the world together against plague corruption.

Palette: deep red, dark steel, white, gold trim.

Faction Two tbd

Current concept: rebel renegades who claim to know the plague's true origin, mistrusted by Ithania and hunted by both authorities and the infected. Name, details, and final direction undecided.

Faction Three tbd

Current concept: a mystic nature-bound order viewing the plague as a natural reckoning rather than a curse. Name, details, and final direction undecided.

Enemies

The enemy concepts below are working drafts. Names and roles may change.

Current visual signature plan: plague creatures marked by orange glowing infection at joints, eyes, and wounds.

The Hollow working

Concept: common shambler, slow, numerous, introductory. Teaches crowd management.

The Skorn working

Concept: fast predator, aggressive, mid-tier. Punishes poor positioning.

The Wretched working

Concept: boss tier, region-defining encounters.

Dragons

Late-game intent. Likely natural and plague-infected variants. Specifics undecided.

Combat

The general feel being aimed for: combat that's fluid but never floaty, with every animation committing — no spam, no mashing. The intent is for the player to think in windows: when to strike, when to dodge, when to hold. Specific systems, mechanics, and balance are very early-stage thinking right now.

Player Verbs (planned)

  • Light attack — quick, chains into combos
  • Heavy attack — slow, breaks defenses
  • Block / parry
  • Roll — i-frames, costs stamina
  • Sprint

Final move list, stamina systems, and weapon variety still being figured out.

Initial Animation Scaffolding

The animations being built right now are early placeholders to get the basic character movement loop running in-engine. Not final combat work — just the minimum needed to shape the foundation.

  • Idle done
  • Walk done
  • Sprint done
  • Roll done

Real combat animations, weapon-specific movesets, and final character feel come much later, after the broader systems are in place.

Rendering Approach

3D geometry rendered at 640×360, scaled up so the scene reads as pixel art. Cel shader on environment, outline shader via depth/normal edge detection. Inspired by t3ssel8r's approach.

Working Pipeline

Character animation: Blender → FBX → Cascadeur → FBX → Godot. FBX throughout — preserves skinning correctly.
